# About Me

**Hello, I'm Erika. 👋**

I like creating things that are beautiful, functional, and accessible. My background is what you can call _multidisciplinary_: design, web dev, data analytics, and even some IT. Add in a splash of customer service experience, my own tech headaches, and genuine curiosity, and a user-focused, iterative process is born.

Outside of the productive things above, I keep up with seasonal anime, play too much Genshin Impact, and enjoy spending time with my family's cats.
